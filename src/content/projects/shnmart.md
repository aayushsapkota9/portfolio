## The problem

SHN Mart needed a fast, modern storefront to sell health, wellness, skincare, baby care, and
medical essentials online. The existing solution wasn't meeting the bar for performance or UX,
and the client needed a frontend that could handle a large product catalogue across multiple
categories without feeling cluttered.

## What I did

Joined as a freelance frontend contractor on the customer-facing side of an existing platform.
The stack had already migrated from React; my scope was the user-visible surface.

- **Bug fixing** — resolved a backlog of reported UI and behaviour issues across the storefront.
- **QA sign-off** — worked through QA requirements to bring the frontend up to the acceptance
  criteria before go-live.
- **Deployment** — deployed the frontend build to the client's VPS.

## Notes

Engagement was scoped to the user-facing side only — admin panel and backend API were outside
my remit. The site is live at shnmart.com.
