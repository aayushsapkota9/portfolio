## The problem

Starlight runs two businesses that share nothing except an owner: a restaurant and a hotel
cleaning service. Prospective customers for one have no interest in the other, and the audience
is split between Japanese and English speakers. One undifferentiated brochure site serves nobody.

## What I built

A bilingual site that keeps the two offerings legible as separate propositions.

- **Japanese-first content** with a genuine English track rather than machine-translated
  fallback — the copy, not just the labels, is written for each audience.
- **Split service presentation** — restaurant and cleaning each get their own narrative and
  gallery, joined only at the company level.
- **Static and fast** — built on Astro so the whole site ships as HTML, which matters on mobile
  networks and keeps hosting costs near zero for a small business.
