## The problem

A restaurant's website has one job: convince someone to come in, then get out of the way while
they book a table. The previous site did neither — it buried the menu, and reservations happened
only by phone during service, which is exactly when nobody can answer.

## What I built

A single-purpose marketing site with booking as the primary action.

- **Food-first landing page** — large imagery and the location ("right next to Ringen Kino")
  above everything else, since proximity is what actually converts a walk-in city audience.
- **Integrated table booking** — party size, date, and time slot collected on-site and handed to
  easyTable, so the restaurant keeps the reservation system its staff already know.
- **Menu, about, and contact** — kept deliberately short and fast on mobile, which is where
  almost all the traffic arrives.
